Financial integrity

  • Integer minor units; basis-point concessions rounded half up.
  • Concurrency-safe invoice/receipt numbers (counter + unique reservation + rules range check).
  • Idempotent payments (id = key) and bulk generation (deterministic invoice ids + line locks).
  • Overpayment rejected in UI, repository and rules; stale concurrent payments refused at commit.
  • Reversal/refund/waiver/void are recorded events tied to invoice changes by rules; receipts retained.
  • Multi-tenant, branch, teacher and parent isolation tested with direct reads/writes.

Known gaps

  • Rules cannot sum invoice lines — a trusted backend is needed to enforce line integrity against a malicious privileged client.
  • No real-Firebase end-to-end run; Dart repositories not exercised against the emulator; indexes unexercised.
  • iOS build not possible here. Single-invoice payments, no drafts, fixed payment methods.
  • Milestone 4 dart format over-reach (disclosed earlier).
