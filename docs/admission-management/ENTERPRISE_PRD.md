# Product requirements (scope delivered so far)

**Problem.** Schools track admission enquiries on paper and spreadsheets: leads are lost,
nobody knows who follows up, duplicates multiply, and there is no audit of why an enquiry
was rejected.

**Users.** School administrators and branch administrators (front office). Parents are not
users of this module yet.

**Delivered.** One place to record an enquiry with a server-generated number, see every
enquiry with search and filters, flag possible duplicates for a human to resolve, move an
enquiry through the lead stages with a reason where required and a permanent history,
assign it to a staff member, and see headline numbers.

**Success measures (to be observed after rollout).** Share of enquiries with a next
follow-up date; share of closed enquiries with a recorded reason; time to first contact
(needs Phase 4); conversion rate as defined in REQUIREMENTS.

**Non-goals now.** Follow-ups/visits, applications, documents, fees, conversion, public
form, notifications, reports, import/export, WhatsApp.

**Principles.** Off by default; additive (no existing collection, route or permission
changes); every write validated on the server; no client-supplied tenant, role, number or
status is ever trusted.
