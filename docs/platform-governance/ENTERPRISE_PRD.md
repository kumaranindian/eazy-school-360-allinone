# Product requirements (summary)

Goal: central governance of school activation, subscriptions, feature availability and operational audit for the multi-tenant EazySchool 360 SaaS.
Users: Avail404 owners, support, compliance, viewers; school administrators (read-only view of their own governance state and notices).
Outcomes: a school cannot be used until activated; access follows lifecycle + subscription + features consistently in every app and backend operation; every important action is attributable, searchable and exportable by authorised platform staff.
Non-goals: pricing/payment collection; redesign of existing screens; per-view read auditing of direct reads; transport/library/online-payment modules (absent from the product).
Success measures: the production-readiness checklist (request section 17) with evidence per item, tracked in `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`; honest known limitations listed (D6-D8).
